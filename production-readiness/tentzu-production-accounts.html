Start these three today: Apple Developer, WhatsApp Business and Stripe. All three wait on third-party verification. Nothing we build shortens those queues, and the launch date is set by them.

Two notes on choices

Document storage — two options. Users upload tenancy contracts; today they are written to the server's own disk and erased on every deployment.

  • Cloudflare R2 — free tier, and no charge for downloads. Storage stays independent of the server, so rebuilding or moving the server cannot lose documents. Recommended.
  • Hostinger VPS disk — included with the VPS at no extra cost, but the contracts then exist in one place only and backups become our responsibility. These are legal documents a tenant may need years later.

Google sign-in is optional; Firebase is not. Firebase is required for Android push and has no substitute. "Sign in with Google" is a separate, optional choice — and if we skip it, Apple's rules no longer oblige us to add "Sign in with Apple" either, removing work from both platforms.

What the company needs to supply

The same details recur across sign-ups — gathering them once avoids repeated delays:

  • Legal entity name, trade licence, registered address
  • D-U-N-S number for the entity, or an application started
  • Business bank account for Stripe payouts
  • Identity documents for the authorised signatory
  • Confirmed public pricing for the Starter and Pro tiers
  • A company email address to own every account — not a personal one. Accounts opened on personal addresses cannot be transferred later, and are a common way businesses lose control of their own app store listing and payment processor.
